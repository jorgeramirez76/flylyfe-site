START WITH A TEE YOU LOVE

Measure. Compare. Choose.

These are garment measurements, not body measurements. Lay a tee flat without stretching it, then compare it with the right chart. The same size letter can feel very different between cuts.

  1. Lay it flat. Smooth out a tee that fits how you like.
  2. Measure the width. Measure straight across the chest. For the relaxed chart, measure one inch below the armhole. This is the flat width, not a chest circumference.
  3. Measure the length. Measure from the high point of the shoulder to the hem; the relaxed chart uses the back hem.

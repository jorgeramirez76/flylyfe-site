FLYLYFE House Music T-Shirt Buying Guide (2026)

This is FLYLYFE’s guide to its own house music, NYC and heritage tees. The catalog has 14 graphic designs across three collection choices: men’s/unisex relaxed, women’s relaxed and women’s fitted. Both relaxed collections use the same Comfort Colors 1717 blank; women’s fitted uses Bella + Canvas 6004, giving two fit and fabric constructions. The 17 entries below cover the 14 designs plus three women’s relaxed examples of designs already listed; they are not 17 different designs. Most designs cost $49.99; Las Malvinas and Las Malvinas Campeones cost $39.99.

What makes a great house music tee

Once you have picked the graphic, our heavyweight graphic tee styling guide walks through tucking, layering and proportions.

Choose by graphic, fabric and fit. Men’s and women’s relaxed tees use heavyweight, garment-dyed Comfort Colors 1717 cotton in S–3XL. Women’s fitted tees use lightweight Bella + Canvas 6004 Airlume cotton in S–2XL and run small. All are printed to order in Los Angeles with a printed inside-neck label. This guide is written by FLYLYFE about its own products, using catalog descriptions and garment specifications. It is not an independent comparison or a wear-test ranking; the numbered entries are browsing aids.

Frequently asked questions

How should I choose a FLYLYFE house music tee?

Start with the graphic you want, then choose relaxed heavyweight cotton or women’s fitted lightweight cotton. The House Music Tee, Anthem and Soul are house-culture designs; NYC and heritage designs are also included in this FLYLYFE brand guide. Compare garment measurements before choosing your size.

What makes a good house music t-shirt?

Look for a graphic you connect with, a fabric weight you prefer and garment measurements that suit you. FLYLYFE offers heavyweight relaxed and lightweight fitted options; neither cut is the right choice for everyone.

Are FLYLYFE house music tees heavyweight and true to size?

Relaxed tees use heavyweight Comfort Colors 1717 cotton in S–3XL. Women’s fitted tees use lightweight Bella + Canvas 6004 cotton in S–2XL and run small. Compare the size guide for your chosen fit with a tee you own. Every tee has a printed inside-neck label.

How much do FLYLYFE house music shirts cost and how fast do they ship?

FLYLYFE ships worldwide — to 200+ countries, every country except Russia and Belarus. US standard shipping is calculated by weight at checkout, from $11.49 for one tee; international shipping is calculated the same way, from $19.49 for one tee, and any import duties, VAT or customs fees charged on delivery are paid by the customer. Production takes 7–10 business days, followed by delivery.

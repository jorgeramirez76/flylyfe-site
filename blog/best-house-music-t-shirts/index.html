What makes a great house music tee

Most "house music" shirts are a stock silhouette of headphones on a cheap blank. FLYLYFE builds the opposite: every tee is printed on the Comfort Colors 1717, a 6.1 oz, 100% ring-spun cotton blank that is garment-dyed after it is sewn, so the color settles into a soft, lived-in finish and the shirt hangs with real weight on the floor. The graphics come from inside the culture — soul manifestos, BPM waveforms, conga rhythms and city coordinates — so the shirt reads to the people who actually understand it. Each one is DTF-printed to order in Los Angeles, cut S to 3XL (women's S to 2XL), and finished with a tagless neck. Here are all 17, ranked for who they're for.

What Is House Music? A Head's Guide to the Sound, the Origins, and the Spirit

House music is electronic dance music built on a four-on-the-floor kick drum, usually 120 to 130 BPM, born in early-1980s Chicago clubs. This guide breaks down its origins, its defining rhythm, its subgenres, and why longtime fans call it a spiritual thing.

NYC house music streetwear runs on a real lineage: Chicago's Warehouse gave the sound its name, and New York's Paradise Garage and loft parties gave it a home. This guide traces that history, breaks down the dark-and-gold aesthetic, and shows how FLYLYFE presses it into wearable cotton.

NYC Subway Token History: From a 1953 Fare Hike to a City Icon

The New York City subway token debuted in 1953, when a fare hike broke the turnstiles, and rode the rails until the MetroCard retired it in 2003. Over those fifty years the little brass coin became a pocket-sized emblem of the city itself.

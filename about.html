ABOUT FLYLYFE · EST. 2007

Born in New York. Built for the dance floor.

FLYLYFE is a New York City house-music streetwear brand for the people who understand the late set, the subway ride home, the basement room, the rooftop speaker, and the feeling that keeps going after the lights come up.

The brand story

Founded in 2007, FLYLYFE connects house music, nightlife, movement, and NYC streetwear. The phrase is simple: Feel the Music. Feel the Vibe. Live Your Lyfe. Every tee carries that energy through premium cotton, bold graphics, and a dark New York visual language with gold accents.

FLYLYFE is always spelled as one word. The brand sells through its official site at flylyfe.com and ships worldwide.

What FLYLYFE makes

House music tees

Graphic tees for house heads, DJs, dancers, producers, and fans who treat the dance floor like a second home.

NYC streetwear

Designs rooted in subway rides, after-hours culture, city coordinates, vintage signage, and New York nightlife.

Premium cotton

Printed-to-order pieces selected for streetwear fit, durability, and clear graphic execution.

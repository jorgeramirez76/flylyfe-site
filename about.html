ABOUT FLYLYFE · EST. 2007

Born in New York. Built for the dance floor.

FLYLYFE is a New York City house-music streetwear brand for the people who understand the late set, the subway ride home, the basement room, the rooftop speaker, and the feeling that keeps going after the lights come up.

The brand story

Founded in 2007, FLYLYFE connects house music, nightlife, movement, and NYC streetwear. The phrase is simple: Feel the Music. Feel the Vibe. Live Your Lyfe. Every tee carries that energy through premium cotton, bold graphics, and a dark New York visual language with gold accents. It started in the city where house music never sleeps.

The brand sells through flylyfe.com and ships worldwide. Say hello at hello@flylyfe.com, @flylyfeofficial on Instagram, or @flylyfe on TikTok.

What FLYLYFE makes

House music tees

Graphic tees for house heads, DJs, dancers, producers, and fans who treat the dance floor like a second home — from The House Music Tee to The Anthem Tee.

NYC streetwear

Designs rooted in subway rides, after-hours culture, city coordinates, vintage signage, and New York nightlife, like The Token Tee.

Premium cotton

Printed-to-order pieces selected for streetwear fit, durability, and clear graphic execution. Most tees are $49.99 USD; the two Las Malvinas heritage tees are $39.99.

How the tees are made

Every tee is printed to order. Production typically takes 7–10 business days, and US delivery usually adds 2–7 business days after the order ships.

The blank

Comfort Colors 1717 — heavyweight, garment-dyed, 100% ring-spun cotton with a structured streetwear drape and a soft washed feel.

The print

DTF (direct-to-film) transfer printing in Los Angeles, USA: full-color, flexible prints that hold detail wash after wash.

The finish

Tagless — a printed inside-neck FLYLYFE label instead of a sewn-in tag. Men's and DROP 02 tees run S–3XL; women's tees, S–2XL.

The collections

Men's

Heavyweight cotton streetwear for house heads, DJs, and dancers, S–3XL: The Signature Tee, The Soul Tee, and The Conga Tee.

Women's

The Anthem, Conga, and Signature designs in a relaxed women's cut, S–2XL.

Drop 02

After-hours sets, 124 BPM, NYC coordinates, and the house-music manifesto: The After Hours Tee, The Tempo Tee, The Coordinates Tee, and The Spiritual Thing Tee.

Limited

Limited-run pieces inspired by New York City graphics, nightlife, and street culture, like The Sanitary Code Tee.

Heritage

Where you're from, worn out loud — hand-lettered graphics printed oversized on garment-dyed cotton: the Las Malvinas Tee and Las Malvinas Campeones Tee.

Concrete Rhythm

Born in the city, moved by the music — hand-drawn New York against real photographed DJ gear, starting with The Brownstone DJ Tee.

Shipping and returns

US standard shipping is $4.75 for most tees; some items use weight-based rates calculated at checkout. US orders of 2 or more items qualify for free standard shipping. Production takes 7–10 business days, followed by delivery. Available destinations and international rates are shown at checkout.

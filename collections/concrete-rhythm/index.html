About Concrete Rhythm

Concrete Rhythm is where FLYLYFE stops illustrating the music and starts photographing it. Every piece in the capsule pairs hand-drawn New York — stoops, wrought iron, the figures who move through it — against real photographed gear: turntables, flight cases, the tools that actually make the night happen. The contrast is the point.

Each tee is printed to order on the heavyweight Comfort Colors 1717, garment-dyed 100% ring-spun cotton, at $49.99 in sizes S–3XL. Back prints run oversized at 14 inches wide. Printed in Los Angeles, USA, and shipped worldwide.

Frequently asked questions

What is FLYLYFE Concrete Rhythm?

Concrete Rhythm is FLYLYFE's NYC street-culture capsule — stoops, side streets, and the walk home after the last record. Its signature move: hand-drawn New York scenes set against real photographed DJ gear like turntables and flight cases. The contrast is the point. Tees are $49.99, sizes S–3XL, on heavyweight garment-dyed cotton.

What is a good t-shirt for a vinyl DJ?

The Brownstone DJ Tee — a crate-digging DJ on a Brooklyn stoop in playful line art, made for people who live behind the decks. It's printed to order on the Comfort Colors 1717 heavyweight blank with an oversized back print running 14 inches wide, $49.99 in sizes S–3XL, shipped from the US.

How big are the prints on Concrete Rhythm tees?

Back prints run oversized at 14 inches wide — the graphic reads like a poster across the shoulders rather than a small chest hit. Everything is DTF-printed to order in Los Angeles on garment-dyed, 100% ring-spun cotton, so the print holds its detail wash after wash while the tee softens.

Does FLYLYFE ship Concrete Rhythm tees internationally?

Yes — FLYLYFE prints in Los Angeles and ships worldwide, with international rates shown at checkout. In the US, shipping is a flat $4.75 for most tees and free on orders of two or more. Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery for unworn, unwashed tees in original condition.

About Heritage

Every piece in Heritage starts as a phrase somebody grew up hearing, drawn by hand rather than set in a typeface. The strokes stay uneven, the layout stays raw, and the graphic runs oversized across the back — the way these words actually show up in the world, on a wall or a banner rather than a brand guide.

Each tee is printed to order on the heavyweight Comfort Colors 1717, garment-dyed 100% ring-spun cotton, at $49.99 in sizes S–3XL, in White, Ivory, and Black. Back prints run 14 inches wide. Printed in Los Angeles, USA, and shipped worldwide.

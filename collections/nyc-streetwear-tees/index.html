Frequently asked questions

What are the best NYC streetwear t-shirts?

FLYLYFE's NYC lineup translates the city into wearable graphics: the Token Tee (subway-token inspiration), the Coordinates Tee (40.7128° N, 74.0060° W), the Sanitary Code Tee (vintage transit signage), the Signature Tee, and the House Music Tee. All are $49.99 on the heavyweight Comfort Colors 1717, printed to order in Los Angeles.

What is the FLYLYFE Token Tee based on?

The NYC subway token — the icon that ran New York transit from 1953 to 2003 — rendered with FLYLYFE's antique-bronze energy. The collection page points readers to the brand's full story of the subway token for the history behind it. Like all FLYLYFE tees, it's DTF-printed to order, $49.99, S–3XL.

Are FLYLYFE NYC tees printed in the USA?

Yes — every tee is garment-dyed Comfort Colors 1717 cotton, DTF-printed to order in Los Angeles, with a tagless printed neck label. Because they're made to order, production runs about 7–10 business days, with US delivery typically adding 1–5 business days. US shipping is $4.75 flat for most tees, free on two or more.

What makes a good New York graphic tee?

By FLYLYFE's definition, one that carries the city with you — the subway token, the coordinates, the vintage signage, the late train home. The brand has printed New York's culture, clubs, slang, and skyline on heavyweight, boxy cotton tees since 2007: downtown attitude you can wear anywhere, shipped worldwide from flylyfe.com.

Three stars, and one date

Three stars means three titles. 1978, 1986, 2022 — that is the whole count, and it is what is printed on the chest, each year set beneath its own star. The 2026 tournament ended differently: Argentina lost the final 1–0 to Spain at MetLife Stadium on July 19. There is no fourth star on this shirt, and there was never going to be. What the tee marks is four days earlier.

On July 15, 2026, in Atlanta, Argentina beat England 2–1 in the semifinal, and players including Giovani Lo Celso and Lisandro Martínez raised a “Las Malvinas son argentinas” banner on the pitch. FIFA later opened disciplinary cases over the banner and over a scuffle after the final. The phrase itself is older than any of it: Spanish for “the Malvinas are Argentine,” Argentina’s sovereignty claim over the South Atlantic islands the United Kingdom administers as the Falklands. The 1982 war ended in British victory and the dispute has never been settled. FLYLYFE prints the line the way it actually lives: brushed by hand, oversized, unpolished.
